Sustainable Data Mining Framework for Regulatory Compliance and Settlement Analytics in Global Financial Markets

📅 Jan 2026📖 IEEE Xplore

This paper introduces the Sustainable Data Mining Framework (SDMF), a cloud-native architecture that integrates big data processing, AI-driven anomaly detection, and energy-efficient computing to strengthen regulatory compliance and settlement analytics in global financial markets. Quantitative experiments benchmarking SDMF against conventional systems demonstrated statistically significant improvements across processing speed, energy consumption, and compliance accuracy. The findings establish that high computational performance and environmental sustainability are not mutually exclusive, offering a viable path toward greater regulatory transparency and ecological responsibility in modern financial data ecosystems.
